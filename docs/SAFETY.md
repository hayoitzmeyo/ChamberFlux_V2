# Safety

This project is experimental.

- It is not a certified air-cleaning or occupational-safety device.
- It does not establish that an enclosure is safe to occupy or open.
- It does not replace appropriate ventilation.
- It does not remove the need to follow filament and printer manufacturer guidance.
- Added airflow may affect chamber temperature, warping, or print quality.
- Use appropriately rated wire, connectors, insulation, and power protection.
- Keep the module outside all printer motion envelopes.
- Do not operate damaged fans or exposed conductors.
- Never rely on odor alone as an indicator of exposure.
