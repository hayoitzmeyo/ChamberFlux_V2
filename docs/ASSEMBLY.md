# Assembly

## Before assembly

Confirm the following before powering the module:

- fan voltage matches the power source,
- wire polarity is correct,
- all conductors are insulated,
- fan blades rotate freely,
- the pivot fasteners do not contact the blades,
- the module does not enter the printer's motion envelope.

## Draft assembly sequence

1. Print the base mount and fan carrier in the documented orientations.
2. Insert the two fans into the carrier.
3. Install the fan fasteners without overtightening the printed frame.
4. Route each fan lead through the integrated grooves and hooks.
5. Join the fan wiring using appropriately rated connectors.
6. Attach the fan carrier at the selected pivot location.
7. Set the angle and secure the pivot hardware.
8. Attach the base using one approved mounting method.
9. Manually move the toolhead and bed through their full travel with power off.
10. Power the fans and confirm airflow direction.
