# Placement Guide

The module is intended to improve chamber-scale circulation, not to cool the printed part directly.

## Recommended starting locations

- upper rear corner,
- upper side wall,
- region opposite the existing filter,
- stagnant zone identified through smoke-free flow visualization or sensor comparison.

## Aiming guidance

Start by aiming airflow:

- across the chamber toward the existing filter intake, or
- along a wall to establish a chamber-scale circulation loop.

Avoid aiming the fan directly at:

- the printed part,
- the build plate,
- loose filament,
- temperature-sensitive components,
- exposed wiring.

## Clearance check

With printer power off, move all axes through their full range and verify clearance to:

- toolhead,
- gantry,
- bed,
- cable chains,
- spool path,
- doors and panels.
